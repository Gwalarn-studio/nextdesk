How the quiz works

Each answer adds points to the jobs that suit that way of working. The result shows your three closest matches. It is a starting point, not a test: many people do well in a job the quiz ranks second.

What to do with your result

  1. Read the first step for your top match and do it this week.
  2. Search the job title plus "remote" on job boards to see real offers.
  3. Rewrite your resume for that job: our remote resume example shows how.

Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored.
