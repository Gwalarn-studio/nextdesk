Cover Letter Examples: 10 Short Letters by Job

A simple four-part structure, then ten short letters by job that you can copy, adapt and send in under twenty minutes.

Updated October 23, 2026
In short
  • A good cover letter is short: about 150 to 250 words, four short paragraphs.
  • Structure: hook, proof, why this company, call to action.
  • Below are ten short letters by job. Copy the closest one and replace every bracket.
  • Always add one detail about the company. That is what makes a letter feel written for them.

Most recruiters skim cover letters. Some read them closely, especially for remote roles and small teams where writing matters. A short, specific letter works for both: the skimmer gets the point in two lines, the careful reader finds real proof.

Below you will find the structure first, then ten examples. They are deliberately short. Use them as a starting point, not as a finished letter: a letter that could be sent to any company convinces no one.

The four-part structure

PartWhat it doesLength
1. HookNames the job and gives your strongest reason in one sentence1–2 sentences
2. ProofOne or two results from your experience that match the ad2–4 sentences
3. Why themShows you know the company: a product, a value, a recent project1–2 sentences
4. Call to actionProposes a next step and thanks the reader1–2 sentences

The hook replaces "I am writing to apply for…". Start with what you bring. The proof should echo the words of the job ad, with numbers if you have them. Why them takes five minutes of research on the company's website. The call to action stays simple: you would like to talk.

Tip: address a real person when you can find the hiring manager's name on the ad or on LinkedIn. If not, "Dear Hiring Manager" or "Dear [Team name] team" is fine.

10 short cover letter examples

1. Customer support

Customer Support Specialist

Dear [Name],

I'd like to join [Company] as a Customer Support Specialist. Helping people solve problems quickly, in clear and friendly writing, is what I do best.

At [Current company], I handle about [50] tickets a day by email and chat, with a satisfaction score of [95%]. I also wrote [20] help articles that reduced repeat questions on [topic].

I use [Product] myself, and I like how your team answers users publicly on [channel]. I'd be glad to bring the same care to your customers.

Could we talk this week or next? Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[First Name Last Name]

2. Sales

Sales Representative

Dear [Name],

I'm applying for the Sales Representative role at [Company]. Over the last [3] years, I've built a pipeline from scratch and closed deals with [type of clients].

At [Current company], I reached [110%] of my annual target and signed [15] new accounts in [region]. I prepare every call carefully and follow up until I get a clear yes or no.

Your expansion into [market] caught my attention, and I know that market well from [experience].

I'd welcome a call to show how I could help your team grow. Thank you for considering my application.

Kind regards,
[First Name Last Name]

3. Administrative assistant

Administrative Assistant

Dear [Name],

I'm writing about the Administrative Assistant position at [Company]. I keep offices running smoothly, so managers can focus on their work.

In my current role at [Company], I manage the calendars of [3] managers, organize [monthly] meetings and process about [100] invoices a month without errors. I created a shared filing system that the whole team now uses.

I'm drawn to [Company] because of [reason: your growth, your sector, your values].

I'd be happy to discuss how I can support your team. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[First Name Last Name]

4. Developer

Software Developer

Hi [Name],

I'm applying for the [Backend / Frontend / Full-stack] Developer role at [Company]. I've spent [4] years building and maintaining [type of product] with [main technologies from the ad].

Recently, I [led the migration of X to Y / cut page load time by 40% / built an API used by Z clients]. I write tests, document my code, and enjoy clear code reviews.

I read your engineering post about [topic], and the way your team approaches [practice] is exactly how I like to work.

My GitHub is [link]. I'd be glad to talk about your stack and the problems you're solving.

Best,
[First Name Last Name]

5. Marketing

Marketing Coordinator

Dear [Name],

I'd love to join [Company] as a Marketing Coordinator. I plan campaigns, write the copy, and measure what works.

At [Current company], I ran our newsletter and social media. In [12] months, the newsletter grew from [2,000] to [6,500] subscribers with an open rate of [38%], and I launched [2] campaigns that brought [result].

Your brand voice on [channel] is clear and human, and I would enjoy contributing to it.

I'd be happy to share a few examples of my work. Thank you for reading.

Best regards,
[First Name Last Name]

6. Nurse or healthcare aide

Nurse / Healthcare Aide

Dear [Name],

I'm applying for the [Nurse / Healthcare Aide] position in your [department] at [Facility]. Patient safety and comfort guide everything I do.

For the past [5] years at [Hospital / care home], I've cared for up to [10] patients per shift, worked closely with doctors and families, and helped train new staff on [procedure or protocol].

Your focus on [patient-centered care / home care / a specific service] matches the way I want to work.

I'm available for [day / night / rotating] shifts and would welcome an interview. Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[First Name Last Name]

7. Retail

Sales Associate

Dear [Store Manager's Name],

I'd like to apply for the Sales Associate position at your [City] store. I enjoy helping customers find what they need, and I stay calm when the store is busy.

At [Current store], I welcome up to [150] customers a day, handle the till and returns, and regularly exceed my monthly sales goals. I also help with stock deliveries and window displays.

I'm a regular customer of [Brand], and I like [products, values, service].

I'm available [days and hours] and would be happy to meet you in store. Thank you!

Best regards,
[First Name Last Name]

8. Teacher

Teacher

Dear [Name],

I'm applying for the [Subject] teacher position at [School]. I help students build confidence as well as knowledge.

Over [6] years teaching [grade levels], I designed [project-based units / a reading program / an after-school club] and saw [concrete progress, e.g. most of my class reach the expected reading level by year end]. I work closely with parents and colleagues.

Your school's [program, value, approach] is something I would be proud to contribute to.

I'd be glad to discuss how I could support your students. Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
[First Name Last Name]

9. Remote job

[Job Title] (Remote)

Hi [Name],

I'm applying for the remote [Job Title] role at [Company]. I've worked remotely for [2] years and I know how to deliver without anyone looking over my shoulder.

At [Current company], I [main result with a number]. Our team spans [3] time zones, so I write clear updates in [Slack / Notion], document my work, and keep meetings short.

I'm based in [City] ([time zone]) and available [hours] in your time zone. I like that your team [async culture, public handbook, product].

Happy to talk whenever suits you. Thanks for reading!

Best,
[First Name Last Name]

10. Career change

[Target Job Title] (career change)

Dear [Name],

After [8] years as a [previous job], I'm moving into [new field], and I'd like to start as [Target Job Title] at [Company].

My past job taught me [transferable skill 1] and [transferable skill 2]: for example, I [concrete result]. To prepare for this change, I completed [course or certificate] and [project], where I [result].

[Company]'s work on [topic] is what made me want to switch fields in the first place.

I'd be grateful for the chance to explain how my background could help your team. Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[First Name Last Name]

How to adapt a letter in 15 minutes

  1. Read the job ad and underline the three requirements that appear most often.
  2. Pick the example above closest to your job.
  3. Replace the proof paragraph with your own results that match those three requirements.
  4. Spend five minutes on the company website and write one specific sentence about them.
  5. Read it aloud, cut any sentence that doesn't add something new, and check the name of the company twice.

Mistakes that cost interviews

  • Repeating your resume. The letter should explain why your experience fits this job, not list it again.
  • Talking only about yourself. One sentence about the company changes the tone of the whole letter.
  • Going too long. If it doesn't fit on half a page, cut.
  • Sending it to the wrong company. Copy-paste errors happen. Check names before sending.
  • Using AI without editing. An AI draft can help you start, but generic phrases are easy to spot. Our ChatGPT prompts for resumes show how to use it well.
