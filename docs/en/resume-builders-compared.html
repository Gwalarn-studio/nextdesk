Online Resume Builders Compared: Free Plans and Prices

Eight resume builders side by side: what the free plan really lets you download, what the paid plans cost, and which subscriptions renew on their own.

Updated October 23, 2026
In short
  • "Free" rarely means the same thing twice. Some builders let you download a PDF for free, others only a text file, others only during a trial.
  • The cheapest serious options are Google Docs templates (free) and the free plans of Novoresume and Kickresume, which include downloads.
  • Watch for low-cost trials that turn into a recurring subscription. Cancel before the trial ends if you do not want to keep paying.
  • For applicant tracking systems (ATS), a simple one-column layout matters more than the tool you use.

How we compared them

We looked at each builder's official pricing or help page in October 2026 and noted four things: what the free plan includes, the paid price, whether the tool promotes ATS-friendly templates, and which languages it supports. We only list a price when it was clearly shown on the official pricing page. Where it was not, the table says "see site".

Prices change often, can depend on your country, and may exclude VAT. Always check the checkout page before you pay. NextDesk has no affiliate deal with any of these tools, and none of the links below earn us money.

The builders one by one

Google Docs templates

The no-frills option. In Google Docs, go to File > New > From a template, or open the Template Gallery, and pick a resume. According to Google's help page, as of October 2026 templates are available in 22 languages, including English, French, Spanish and German.

Good for: anyone who wants a clean, text-based resume and full control. You can export to Word or PDF. Limits: few designs, and you do the layout yourself. No writing help beyond what you add.

Canva

Canva is a design tool, not a resume specialist. Its resume templates page says the templates are free and can be downloaded in PDF, JPG or PNG. As of October 2026, the Canva pricing page lists the free plan at US$0 and Pro at US$144 per year for one person (US prices; your local price may differ). The page offers a free trial of Pro, but does not state on the main table what happens when it ends, so check the terms before you start one.

Good for: creative roles, where a visual resume is expected. Limits: many templates use columns, icons and text boxes. These look great to a human, but some ATS software reads them badly. If you apply through online forms, choose the plainest template.

Novoresume

As of October 2026, the Novoresume pricing page lists a free Basic plan (one version, one-page resume, predefined layouts, no cover letter) and Premium at US$23.99 for one month, US$49.99 for three months or US$199.99 for one year. The page states "Pay once. No recurring billing" and "There Is No Automatic Subscription", with a refund available in the first 14 days. The homepage says the Basic plan includes a free PDF download.

Good for: a free one-page resume with a modern design, and anyone who hates subscriptions. Limits: the free version is capped at one page, and downloading a resume that uses Premium features is not possible on the Basic account.

Kickresume

As of October 2026, the Kickresume pricing page lists a free plan with 4 basic resume templates, matching cover letter templates and unlimited downloads, with limited fonts and colors. Paid plans are US$24 per month, US$54 every 3 months, or US$96 every 12 months, with a 14-day money-back guarantee. The page does not say clearly whether paid plans renew automatically, so check at checkout. The homepage promotes ATS-friendly templates and an ATS resume checker.

Good for: a free resume you can actually download, with room to upgrade. Limits: the free designs are basic.

Zety

As of October 2026, the Zety pricing page (US site) lists a free Basic plan with unlimited edits, but free downloads are in TXT format only. PDF and Word downloads require a paid plan. The Pro plan costs US$1.95 for a 14-day trial, then renews automatically at US$25.95 every 4 weeks. The Annual plan costs US$71.40 upfront and renews each year. Both can be cancelled at any time.

Good for: guided writing with pre-written suggestions. Limits: the low trial price hides a recurring charge. If you only need one resume, set a reminder to cancel before day 14.

Resume.io

The Resume.io pricing page did not show plan prices when we checked in October 2026, so we cannot quote one. It does state that if you do not cancel during the 7-day premium trial, you are automatically subscribed to the premium monthly membership, and that there is a 7-day money-back guarantee. The homepage mentions a free template and PDF download, but does not say whether the download is free.

Good for: a quick, clean resume if you are fine with a short paid trial. Limits: unclear free download; auto-renewal after the trial.

Enhancv

As of October 2026, the Enhancv pricing page describes a free plan valid for 7 days, with all templates but Enhancv branding and a limit of 12 section items. The paid Pro plan adds an ATS check, content suggestions and no branding. The price fields on the page did not display correctly when we checked, so see the site for current pricing. Resumes download as PDF, and a translation feature covers 9 languages, including English, French, German and Spanish.

Good for: modern designs and content suggestions. Limits: the free plan is time-limited and branded.

CVDesignR

A French builder. As of October 2026, the CVDesignR Premium page lists a free plan with up to 8 resumes and free templates, and a Premium plan that adds an ATS score, AI suggestions and advice based on the job ad. The page itself warns that the displayed price must be checked before buying, so see the site. The site is available in French, English, Spanish, German, Italian and Dutch.

Good for: applying in France or in French. Limits: the pricing page does not explain renewal terms clearly.

Subscriptions and free downloads: read the small print

Resume builders often let you build your resume for free, then ask you to pay at the download step. That is not illegal, but it can feel like a trap after an hour of work. Before you start, check three things:

  1. Can I download a PDF for free? Not a TXT file, not a watermarked version: a real PDF.
  2. Does the trial renew automatically? A 14-day trial for a small fee can become a monthly charge.
  3. How do I cancel? Look for the cancellation steps in the account settings or help page before you enter a card.
Tip: if you subscribe for one application campaign, cancel right after your last download. Check in the terms whether you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

If you need a resume today and do not want to pay, start with free resume templates or Google Docs. Your words matter more than the tool.

Which one should you choose?

  • Zero budget, ATS-safe: Google Docs, with a simple one-column template.
  • Free and designed: Novoresume (one page) or Kickresume (basic templates).
  • Creative role: Canva, keeping a plain version for online forms.
  • No subscription: Novoresume, which states it uses one-off payments.
  • Applying in French: CVDesignR, or a Google Docs template in French.
